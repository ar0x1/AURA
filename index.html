Frequently Asked Questions

Is AURA free?

Yes. AURA is free and open source under the MIT-0 license. It includes free chat via AURA ENGINE and free text-to-speech via AURA TTS, so there is nothing to pay for and no trial to expire. If you are my close one then u can get more then free 😉.

Do I need an account to use AURA?

No account is required to chat with AURA! Open the web-app and start chatting immediately.

Does AURA work on a phone?

Yes!. AURA runs in the mobile browser with a layout built for small screens. There is no app to install on Android or iOS.

Does my AI waifu remember our conversations?

Yes. AURA keeps conversation memory but only on the device which you are using. Clearing the history also affects, specially for some people 😏, so your companion stays consistent between sessions. Memory lives in your browser, not on a server.
